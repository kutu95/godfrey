# SS Georgette — van Zeller scholarly synthesis (live context)

Source: Marcia van Zeller, doctoral thesis, Curtin University, 2015. **Exegesis and author's note only.**  
The novel *Cruel Capes* in the same PDF is **fiction**. Never use it for a name, boat, beach, count, or death.

This file is what the live model may retrieve. `docs/verified-facts.md` still outranks it. Where the two differ, follow verified-facts.

Godfrey speaks in early 1877. He does not know later letters, medals, books, or anyone's life after that year.

## The popular story (false as a sequence)

Later retellings run the wreck as one scene at Calgardup (modern Redgate — never say Redgate in character): the boat launched to the beach, women and children drowning in that surf, Grace Bussell and Sam Isaacs swimming horses out to the ship for hours and hauling everyone in.

That is the plaque story. It is not what happened.

---

## What the sources she used actually show

**Two landings, not one.**

1. Still miles offshore (about twenty miles, Leeuwin sand patch in sight) the **lifeboat** was filled mainly with women and children, stove under the counter, and capsized. People died **in open water**, not in the Calgardup surf. Verified-facts: **seven** from that boat; the eighth, Herbert Osborne, died **aboard** the ship. Van Zeller sometimes says “eight lives out to sea.” Use the verified split, not that shorthand.

2. The **gig** could not get back to the steamer. It came ashore at **Injidup** with **fourteen** people, all named on Dundee’s police list of 3 December 1876 (verified-facts F-106a). Then those who could walked inland through the bush at night for help. It did **not** land at Calgardup.

3. **Later**, the Georgette was run ashore at **Calgardup Bay**. The **pinnace** was the only boat still with her. The crew ran her on two ropes, ship to beach and back, for hours. She capsized in the surf more than once. **No one died on that landing.** About **fifty** remaining aboard came ashore that way (Leake). Walter Geddes told the inquiry it was done with difficulty but without loss of life.

**Annie Simpson** travelled steerage with her infant. She was put in the lifeboat; after it went down she was among those taken into the **gig**. She and the child came ashore at **Injidup**, not in the pinnace at Calgardup. Settlers inland of that gig landing (Harwood, Abbey, Guerrier, Yelverton, the McGregors of Quindalup among them) took the party in. Godfrey may name those settlers if asked. He must not quote her later letters, and he must not say he saw her on Calgardup beach.

**Grace Bussell and Sam Isaacs** rode down to Calgardup and helped people from the surf. Credit them. Do not make them the whole rescue. Five of the crew took a line; the first breakers capsized the pinnace; they hauled her back and forth on two ropes through about fifty yards. **No one died on that landing.** Leake (and James Lambe to the Colonial Secretary) said the horses were not in the heavy breakers the English papers described. The inquiry barely mentioned the riders; it was about the lifeboat and the leak.

If Godfrey praises anyone for the Calgardup landing, it is **his own men** in the pinnace. Willie Dempster saved the **gig** when her after fall carried away, still miles offshore — that is a different boat and a different beach.

## The dead (names)

Use verified-facts F-114. In short:

From the lifeboat — two women and five children: Mrs Davis and Alexander Davis (9); Mrs Elizabeth Hauxwell and John (2), Frances (8), Isabella (4); Ada Dixon (8).

Aboard the ship: Herbert Osborne (8).

Harriet Dixon, Ada's mother, survived and came ashore in the **gig** with a surviving infant. Annie Simpson and her infant likewise. Miss Welche and Mrs Stammers with two children complete the four women and four children.

## Invented in the novel — never speak as fact

Van Zeller states that she invented: a letter from Captain John Molloy to Alfred Bussell; a funeral eulogy by Henry Simpson in 1934 in St Paul, Minnesota; and some passages that look like quotations from records. None of that is in Godfrey's knowledge. He does not know what became of Annie in America.

## Do not invent

The gig's fourteen are named (F-106a / `godfrey-gig-manifest-1876.md`). There is still no complete named list of the fifty at Calgardup (D-108). If a visitor wants every Calgardup name, say you cannot recall them all. Do not guess a beach from the fact that someone survived.
