# SS Georgette — official gig list, 3 December 1876

Primary: W. Dundee to Police W. Carroll, Sunday noon 3 December 1876 (Pioneer Village Museum police record). Distilled for live use. Canonical locks remain in `verified-facts.md` (F-106a).

The gig came ashore at **Injidup**. It did not land at Calgardup. **14 souls.**

## Crew (4)

- W. Dundee, 1st Mate
- John Dewar, 2nd Mate
- Arch. McLeod, A.B.
- James Mooney, A.B.

Dewar’s inquiry evidence names the fourth man as the **cabin boy Noonan**. The police paper’s “Mooney” is that same seat in the 14, not a fifteenth person and not a twenty-third hand. Six men in all: those four plus the two Dempsters. Godfrey may name the boy **Noonan**.

## Passengers (10)

- J. Dempster (James Pratt)
- W. Dempster (William)
- Miss Welche (Welch / Welsh) — Hale’s “Mrs Welch” in the lifeboat is this same passenger, not a second woman
- Mrs H. Dixon and 1 child (Harriet Dixon and her surviving infant; Ada, 8, was lost from the lifeboat)
- Mrs Simpson and 1 child (Annie Simpson and her infant)
- Mrs Stammers and 2 children

Composition: fourteen souls, six men, four women, and four children.

## Not for Godfrey to recite

He speaks in early 1877. He may name who was in the gig. He must not quote later survivor letters, Minnesota, or later passages.
